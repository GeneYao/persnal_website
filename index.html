熱愛有機、富有質地和靈魂的聲響，廣泛遊走在各種不同的音樂類型之間，以敏銳的選曲與細膩的編排，為空間裡的光線與空氣注入各式色彩，讓音樂的能量帶領你回歸本質，踏上一段模糊時間與空間邊界的沉浸式聽覺體驗。

A love for organic, textured, soulful sound that moves freely across genres. Through sharp selection and careful arrangement, music brings color to the light and air of a space, and its energy returns everything to its essence, through an immersive listening experience that blurs the edges of time and space.
