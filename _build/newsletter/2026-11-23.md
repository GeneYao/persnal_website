## 2. Dirty Work
**Steely Dan**<br>*Soft Rock · Yacht Rock*

![Dirty Work](https://geneyao.com/photos/songs/steely-dan-dirty-work.jpg)

## 3. Wait
**Earth, Wind & Fire**<br>*R&B · Soul*

![Wait](https://geneyao.com/photos/songs/earth-wind-fire-wait.jpg)

## 4. Band On The Run
**Paul McCartney & Wings**<br>*Rock · Pop Rock*

![Band On The Run](https://geneyao.com/photos/songs/paul-mccartney-wings-band-on-the-run.jpg)

一直很喜歡 Paul McCartney 這張的音樂，有股讓人很放鬆的煙草味。這首歌有搖滾、有抒情，又有點鄉村，非常精彩。
