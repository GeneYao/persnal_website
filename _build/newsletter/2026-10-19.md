## 1. Song of Injured Love

## 2. I'd Be Lost
**Thee Marloes**

![I'd Be Lost](https://geneyao.com/photos/songs/thee-marloes-id-be-lost.jpg)

得知他們要來台灣巡演，一聽超喜歡，帶有細膩的 Motown 律動，但又有印尼的陽光熱情。

I heard they are coming to Taiwan on tour, and one listen made me love them. It has a delicate Motown groove, yet also the sunny warmth of Indonesia.

[在 YouTube 收聽 ↗](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9zR8uaiXK8o)

## 3. Hanen
**Carthago**

![Hanen](https://geneyao.com/photos/songs/carthago-hanen.jpg)

在 IG 聽到別人分享的突尼西亞 Disco，馬上就被這律動吸引，非常有趣好聽。

I heard this Tunisian disco track shared by someone on Instagram, and I was instantly drawn in by the groove. It is so much fun and sounds great.

[在 YouTube 收聽 ↗](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAngN0DqwZ8)

## 4. One Of These Things First
**Nick Drake**

![One Of These Things First](https://geneyao.com/photos/songs/nick-drake-one-of-these-things-first.jpg)

開車從嘉義北上時聽的音樂。一直很喜歡 Nick Drake 的音樂，單純卻有深度。

This was the music for my drive north from Chiayi. I have always loved Nick Drake, simple yet deep.

[在 YouTube 收聽 ↗](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vDtsgVgAx6k)

## 5. Kaze
**Ebi**

![Kaze](https://geneyao.com/photos/songs/ebi-kaze.jpg)

一直很喜歡橫田進的 Ambient 作品，意外在一場活動聽到這首，經過詢問後才知道他有其他化名，做不同風格的音樂。

I have always loved Susumu Yokota's ambient work. I heard this one by chance at an event, and after asking around, I learned that he has other aliases for making music in different styles.

[在 YouTube 收聽 ↗](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Ebi+Kaze+Space+Teddy)
