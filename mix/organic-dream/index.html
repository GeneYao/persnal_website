Organic Dream @ ResonanceXone 20260110

2026.01.10 SAT · ResonanceXone · 桃園 2026.01.10 SAT · ResonanceXone · Taoyuan

音樂在山裡慢慢展開，
陪著光線移動，
節奏從土地裡浮現，
午後空氣的仍亮，
腳步在山林間上來回，
身體順著節奏微微回應。

Music slowly unfolds in the mountains,
moving with the light,
rhythm emerging from the earth,
the afternoon air still bright,
footsteps wandering through the forest,
the body responding gently to the beat.

音樂只是場景的一部分，
與樹影、雲層、風聲、談話聲一起存在，
在山裡，慢慢完成。

Music is only one part of the scene,
existing alongside tree shadows, clouds, wind and conversation,
slowly taking shape in the mountains.
